E-COMMERCE BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT
PROJECT PLAN

1. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

Develop an interactive E-Commerce Business Intelligence dashboard that helps management monitor sales and profitability, identify important sales trends and business drivers, evaluate regional, product, and customer-segment performance, identify risks and opportunities, and support data-driven business decisions.


2. KEY BUSINESS QUESTIONS

KPIs — What is happening?
- What are the total sales?
- What is the total profit?
- How many orders have been placed?
- How many units have been sold?
- What is the average order value?
- What is the overall profit margin?

Trends — Where are we heading?
- How are monthly sales changing over time?
- How is monthly profit changing over time?
- How is order volume changing over time?

Drivers — Why is performance changing?
- Which regions generate the most sales and profit?
- Which countries generate the most sales and profit?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which products generate the most sales and profit?
- How do different customer segments perform?
- Which payment methods are most frequently used?

Risks & Opportunities
- Which products or categories have low profitability?
- Are there areas with high sales but relatively low profit?
- Which areas have high shipping costs?
- How do discounts relate to sales and profit?

Actions
- Identify products and categories that require management attention.
- Identify regions and countries with strong or weak performance.
- Investigate areas where profitability can be improved.
- Use dashboard insights to support inventory, pricing, discount, and business strategy decisions.


3. CORE KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin


4. MAIN ANALYSIS DIMENSIONS

- Order Date
- Customer Segment
- Country
- Region
- Product Category
- Product Name
- Payment Method


5. PROJECT OUTPUT

Create an interactive E-Commerce Business Intelligence dashboard with:
- KPI cards
- Sales and profit trend charts
- Regional and country analysis
- Product and category analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Risk and opportunity analysis
- Interactive filters
- Business insights and recommended management actions
